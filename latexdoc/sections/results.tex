\section{Results}

Validation was performed on 124 wound-bearing phase-contrast images drawn from 134
baseline frames acquired across two cell lines (iMC~ISOR544C and iMC~MUTR544C) and
two independent experiments (EXP1, $n = 62$; EXP2, $n = 72$).
Ten images (7.5\%) were excluded for containing no visible wound at $t = 0$;
9 of 10 occurred in EXP1 and 8 of 10 in the iMC~MUTR544C line
(Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp-nowound-distribution}). Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:tool-comparison} contextualises the pipeline against established and recent
alternatives. 

\subsection{Wound Detection Accuracy}

Of 124 wound-bearing images, the pipeline correctly segmented 122 (98.4\%) as judged
by expert review.
Only 2 images were incorrectly segmented, both in the iMC~MUTR544C line in EXP1.
Performance was consistent across conditions and cell lines (Table~\ref{tab:accuracy}), and
reproducible across experiments (EXP1: 51/53, 96.2\%; EXP2: 71/71, 100.0\%).

\subsection{Automated Quality-Control Performance}

Automated QC achieved 98.5\% agreement with the human reviewer across all annotated
images ($n = 134$; Cohen's $\kappa = 0.90$;
Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp-qc-confusion}).
The module identified all 122 correctly segmented images (sensitivity~=~1.00, NPV~=~1.00)
and flagged 10 of 12 problematic images as invalid (specificity~=~0.83).
The two false positives --- incorrectly segmented images that passed QC --- were
indistinguishable from valid segmentations by variance-based scoring alone.
Within the wound-bearing cohort ($n = 124$), the pipeline correctly classified 123 of 124
images (accuracy~=~99.2\%) without false negatives.

\subsection{Wound Closure Dynamics}

After QC validation, 59 trajectories with a cross-sectional measurement at the final
timepoint entered the wound closure dynamics analysis: DMSO ($n = 23$), Media ($n = 21$),
ALK5i ($n = 15$), across two cell lines: iMC~ISOR544C ($n = 33$) and
iMC~MUTR544C ($n = 26$). Each trajectory comprised 20--25 timepoints (Figure~\ref{fig:fig1}).

By construction, the temporal constraint produced strictly non-increasing wound-area
trajectories as depicted in Figure~\ref{fig:fig1}D and in Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp-qc-validation}. 
Across the 59 QC-validated trajectories, mean normalized closure reached 11.8\%
($\pm$~0.8\% SEM) at 12~hours and 15.3\% ($\pm$~0.9\% SEM) at 20~hours, with the ALK5i
condition lowest at both timepoints
(Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp-closure}).

Retaining the six trajectories that failed $t = 0$ QC --- four in the ALK5i group in the
iMC~MUTR544C line --- introduced spurious masks that inflated apparent ALK5i closure to
31.0\% ($\pm$~8.5\% SEM) and inverted the condition ranking relative to the
edge-displacement metric.
Restricting to QC-validated trajectories removed this artifact and reconciled the
area-based and displacement-based metrics.

Wound closure trajectories followed a characteristic pattern across all conditions
(Figure~\ref{fig:fig2}): an initial lag phase (0--4~h) with minimal closure, followed by
progressive inward displacement that did not plateau within 24~hours.
Per-condition mean trajectories separated with non-overlapping 95\% confidence bands
from 12~h onward, demonstrating that the pipeline resolves condition-dependent
closure differences at the scale of this cohort.

Cross-sectional analysis at the final timepoint yielded no statistically significant
pairwise differences after Bonferroni correction across both cell lines
(Line~1: ALK5i vs.\ DMSO $p = 0.38$, $d = -0.40$; Line~2: $p = 0.40$, $d = -0.29$;
full results in Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp-pairwise}).
Media did not differ from DMSO in either cell line ($p > 0.59$).
Corrected $p$-values of 1.000 reflect per-group sample sizes of $n = 5$--15 and a
mean closure range of ${\sim}20\%$ over 24~h, not a failure of the pipeline to resolve
differences of this magnitude.

A linear mixed-effects model (Equation~\eqref{eq:mixed-model}) confirmed a significant
main effect of time ($\beta = 0.880$~px/frame, $p < 10^{-12}$), establishing that wound
closure proceeded consistently across all conditions.
No time $\times$ condition or time $\times$ cell line interaction reached significance
(all $p > 0.31$), consistent with the cross-sectional findings.
Full sample accounting, QC pass rates, descriptive statistics, pairwise comparisons with
confidence intervals, and inter-experiment reproducibility are provided in
Supplementary Tables~\ref{tab:supp-sample-sizes}--\ref{tab:supp-mixed-model-coefficients}.
