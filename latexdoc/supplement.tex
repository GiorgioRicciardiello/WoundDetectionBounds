\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Pipeline parameter values and Kalman initialisation details.
  All parameters are fixed across experiments and cell lines; none require user tuning.
  $P(x,0)$ initialisation: initial uncertainty is estimated as
  $P(x,0) = \max(R_\text{base}(0),\, 1.0)$, where
  $R_\text{base}(0) = \operatorname{Var}_x(u_\text{RANSAC}(x,0) - u_\text{det}(x,0))$
  is the within-frame spatial residual variance between RANSAC-rejected and
  Savitzky--Golay smoothed edges at $t = 0$.
  Fallback: if fewer than 6 finite-valued columns are available or fallback detection
  is active at $t = 0$, $R_\text{base}(0) = 0.1 \times Q_0 = 40$~px$^2$.
  The floor of 1.0 prevents zero initial uncertainty from collapsing the Kalman gain.
  Numerical safeguards: (i) non-finite $R$ clamped to $Q_0$;
  (ii) non-finite Kalman output reset to previous edge;
  (iii) $P$ reset to $P^-$ when hard safety net fires;
  (iv) last-resort revert if $A(t) > A(t-1)$ persists after all constraint paths.}
\label{tab:pipeline-parameters}
\small
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
Stage & Parameter & Value & Purpose \\
\midrule
Variance map        & Window size $w$                          & 15~px         & Local texture estimation \\
Y-band              & Row-profile Gaussian $\sigma_y$          & 10.0~px       & Wound centre smoothing \\
Y-band              & Variance percentile threshold            & 35th          & Sub-threshold band selection \\
Y-band              & Margin expansion fraction                & 0.15          & Band boundary padding \\
Edge extraction     & Threshold factor                         & 0.5           & $\tau = 0.5 \cdot \text{median}(\sigma^2_\text{cell})$ \\
RANSAC              & Polynomial degree                        & 2             & Captures genuine curvature \\
